# Say it

The user has chosen practice. The heard word and the intended word are in their message, or in the turn they point at.

1. Write the intended word as sounds they can copy. Use a word they already say for the sound that was dropped or swapped.
2. One sentence on where the heard word and the intended word split.
3. End with the dictionary entry for that word and the spoken forms it already has. This heard word stays off that list.

Done when the reply has one cue for the next take, and no settings file was written.

Example. Heard "codec", intended "Codex": say "code-eks", with a clear "ks" at the end. "Codec" ends like "deck".
